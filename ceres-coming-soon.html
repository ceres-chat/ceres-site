Ceres · ceres.chat

Voice chat that
belongs to no one.

I'm building Ceres because I got tired of my conversations living on someone else's servers.

Ceres is fully peer-to-peer voice and text chat — no company infrastructure in the path of your voice, no accounts, no surveillance. Your conversations travel directly between the people having them.

It's early. A small group of friends are helping me test it. If you believe your conversations are your own business — this is being built for you.
